About

Ads rarely fail alone.

I’m a performance marketer from Delhi NCR. For nearly four years I’ve run Meta and Google ads for luxury fashion, healthcare, education, hospitality and D2C brands. The pattern I keep finding: the ad is rarely the only problem. Tracking is broken, the page leaks, or nobody follows up on the lead.

So I work the whole funnel with the ad at the centre. Most recently I was Digital Marketing Manager at GG Studio, a luxury couture house. Before that, nearly four years at AB Media, taking on everything from lead generation for clinics to app installs at crore-level spend.
