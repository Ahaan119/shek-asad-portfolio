The complaint

The platform advertised only in India, on Meta, with a thin funnel. Expanding to the Middle East and US meant CPCs 3–4x higher and different buyers in each market.

Diagnosis

  • Copy that worked for Indian parents didn't carry across markets.
  • Paid alone would get more expensive as spend scaled.

Work done

  • Scaled Meta across new markets with localised creative: family-centred for the Middle East, skill-building and global competition for the US.
  • Paired paid media with SEO and PR to build organic authority.
