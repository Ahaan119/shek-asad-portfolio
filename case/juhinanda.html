The complaint

Earlier campaigns often returned under 1x in slow months, so the brand stopped advertising. Sales were uneven across seasons, reporting under-counted purchases because of lost data signal, and Cash on Delivery had been disabled.

Diagnosis

  • The platform was optimising on incomplete data, so delivery and measurement were both off.
  • Prospecting without strong remarketing left high-intent visitors unconverted.
  • A high-ticket brand needs a funnel, not one campaign.

Work done

  • Fixed the Conversions API and event matching to recover signal quality.
  • Built precise audiences and a strong remarketing layer.
  • Added creator-led content so performance held through the low season.

  • Attract

  • Compound
