The complaint

Meta lead-form ads were producing expensive leads. Details reached the calling team through Excel sheets, many leads were unqualified, and prescriptions and doctor bookings were delayed.

Diagnosis

  • Lead forms invite low-intent taps, so cost per lead looked fine while sales time was wasted.
  • The manual Excel handoff slowed follow-up, and slow follow-up kills intent.
  • Nothing filtered leads before they reached the sales team.

Work done

  • Rebuilt the funnel on Click-to-WhatsApp ads across Facebook and Instagram.
  • Added a structured qualification step in the chat, before any lead reached sales.
  • Routed qualified leads straight into Zoho CRM with Zapier automation, with follow-ups automated.
  • Set up GA4 and GTM tracking so spend could be tied to qualified leads, not just clicks.

  • Compound
