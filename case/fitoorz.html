The complaint

Stuck at an inconsistent ~4x ROAS that dipped after a few days of continuous delivery. Ad fatigue and 'Learning Limited' status kept Meta delivery uneven and growth capped despite demand.

Diagnosis

  • Creative wore out faster than it was replaced.
  • Audiences overlapped across stages, which fragmented learning.

Work done

  • Rebuilt the account as a top, middle and bottom-of-funnel structure.
  • Set up custom audiences for each stage.
  • Created a systematic creative testing and refresh system to keep delivery stable.
