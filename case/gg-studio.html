The complaint

A luxury house where the sale closes in a boutique or on WhatsApp, not at checkout, so platform ROAS alone undercounts what ads deliver. Ads, site, local listings and search all needed to work as one system.

Diagnosis

  • Online tracking sees a small slice of a high-ticket, assisted sale.
  • Search was shifting to AI answers the brand wasn't prepared for.

Work done

  • Ran Meta and Google Ads, with budget decisions grounded in sales rather than platform clicks.
  • Built Shopify, Mailchimp and WhatsApp automation flows.
  • Managed seven boutique Google Business Profile listings.
  • Led technical and on-page SEO across 380+ pages on two storefronts.
  • Built the brand's first GEO/AEO programme: tracking its visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, and fixing the gaps.
