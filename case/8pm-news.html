The complaint

Cost per install was too high to scale, and spend was leaking into audiences that would never use the app, including users in dry states and under-24s.

Diagnosis

  • Targeting was too broad for a niche content series.
  • Installs were counted, not retention, so quantity hid quality.

Work done

  • Tightened geography and age targeting.
  • Ran a dual structure: Google app campaigns for install volume and YouTube video for brand awareness around a defence and patriotic content series.
  • Used Firebase retention data to judge installs by engaged users.
