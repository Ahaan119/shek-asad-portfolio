Higher education

Call-only ads: 70% cheaper leads and 3x the calls.

The sale closes via

The complaint

Forms and landing pages made students drop off before they spoke to a counsellor, and timing mattered: the window was before results were announced.

Diagnosis

  • Friction between interest and conversation lost the intent.
  • Calls were the real conversion, so calls had to be measured.

Work done

  • Launched call-only campaigns timed before the results window.
  • Set up call tracking and measured qualified call duration, not just call count.
